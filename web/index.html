How this text was generated

Sentences are composed by a random grammar: twelve sentence frames (statements, questions, commands, compound, subordinate and relative clauses…) with independently optional modifiers, varied tense and person. Each sentence is the best of several candidates, penalising word sequences that appeared in earlier recordings and frames already used in this test. Seed and frames are saved with the session.

180 Hz high-pass → spectral flux (1 ms frames) ÷ its 251 ms running median. Peaks above are candidates.

How the metrics are defined
            

              
Detection
Detected presses matched one-to-one to true presses within 20 ms (v6's rule). Recall = matched / typed; precision = matched / detected.

              
Key acc. (detected)
Of matched presses, the share named correctly. Audio-only, end to end.

              
Key acc. (logged timing)
The CNN run at the true press positions (auto-repeats excluded): the classifier on its own, separate from detection. Balanced = mean per-key recall.

              
CER / WER
Levenshtein distance between the lowercase typed text and the predicted text, divided by the typed length, in characters / words.

              
Word confidence
Softmax of the decoder's scores over its best candidate words for that stretch of presses.
