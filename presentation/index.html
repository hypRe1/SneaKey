DECODED

“Random guessing gets 4%. On real typing we get about half. When a key is heard cleanly, 4 out of 5, and 9 out of 10 if we allow three guesses.” Why the gap: about half of key presses are too quiet for the laptop mic, and fast typing makes sounds overlap. SNK-006 // CountermeasuresEyes only

How to stay unheard

01 · MUTE

Mute while you type

On calls, especially passwords and private notes.

02 · FILTER

Turn on noise suppression

Zoom, Teams and Discord can filter out keyboard sounds.

03 · AUTOFILL

Use a password manager

Passwords get filled in, not typed.

04 · LOOK AROUND

Watch for microphones nearby

A phone on the table can listen too.

Keep it short. Judges like projects that also explain how to defend against them.
